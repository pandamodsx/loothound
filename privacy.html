Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Playfever Labs LLC ("we", "us") collects through the Loothound app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Loothound is intended for users 18 and older.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and sign-in details (including Google sign-in if you choose it).
  • Steam information: the Steam trade URL you provide (which includes your Steam account ID), used to send items you redeem. If you use the Steam name bonus, we read your public Steam profile name through the Steam Web API each time you check it.
  • Activity in the app: points earned and spent, offers completed, items redeemed, referral codes, and promo codes used.
  • Device and technical data: device identifiers (including advertising ID), IP address, device model, OS version, and app diagnostics. We use these to run the app and to prevent fraud.

Offer and advertising partners

Offers and ads in Loothound are provided by third-party offerwall and advertising networks. When you open an offerwall or watch an ad, those partners may collect device identifiers and information about the offers you engage with, under their own privacy policies. They tell us when you complete an offer so we can credit your points. We do not sell your personal information.

Video ads are provided by Appodeal and Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location (from IP) to show and measure ads. See their privacy policies at those links.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep your points balance.
  • To deliver items you redeem to your Steam account.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and multiple accounts.
  • To provide support and improve the app.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Service providers

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) for authentication, data storage, and server functions. Data may be processed in the United States.

Retention

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is active and as needed for fraud prevention, accounting, and legal requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your account data by emailing 859djmayes@gmail.com. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap the hound in the top-left corner (Your den), scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account. If you can't open the app, email 859djmayes@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it within 30 days.

Deleting removes your profile, your points and trade-in credit balances, coupons, Hunt List and settings, and your sign-in. Skins that are still on their way must arrive or be cancelled first. We keep your point history and past deliveries without your email or trade URL, for fraud prevention and accounting, along with a one-way fingerprint of your email and your Steam account ID so deleting an account can't be used to get around fraud checks.

Children

Loothound is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app.
